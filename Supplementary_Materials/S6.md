# S6. Artificial Intelligence Risk-Layer Evaluation

## S6.1 Role of the risk layer

For each proposed action, the deterministic authorization check first establishes whether the action has the current authority it needs for execution. Only actions that pass enter the risk evaluation. Within this authorized population, a Gradient Boosting classifier assigns additional treatment using the same configuration throughout the experiment. Thus, the model relates the set of actions retained after risk treatment to the set admitted by deterministic authorization as follows.

$$
A_t^{risk}\subseteq A_t^{auth}.
$$

## S6.2 Authorized population

After deterministic authorization, the experiment places each case in one of two groups, namely, the controlled high-risk group and the remaining authorized group. The two groups have no cases in common. For each group, the number of authorized cases provides the denominator for measuring how often an action receives additional treatment. For the controlled population as a whole and for its two groups, the case counts are as follows.

| Population | Authorized cases |
|---|---:|
| Total | 90,000 |
| Controlled high-risk group | 42,837 |
| Remaining authorized group | 47,163 |

## S6.3 Treatment metrics

For the controlled high-risk group and the remaining authorized group, the Risk Treatment Rate (RTR) and Friction Introduction Rate (FIR) measure the frequency of additional treatment, respectively. Each denominator counts all authorized cases in the corresponding group. Within that group, the numerator counts only the authorized cases for which the classifier assigns additional treatment. For the authorized cases assigned to the high-risk group and those in the remaining group, the two treatment rates are defined as follows.

$$
\mathrm{RTR}=
\frac{N_{\text{high-risk authorized cases receiving treatment}}}
{N_{\text{high-risk authorized cases}}},
$$

$$
\mathrm{FIR}=
\frac{N_{\text{remaining authorized cases receiving treatment}}}
{N_{\text{remaining authorized cases}}}.
$$

## S6.4 Results with and without the risk layer

As shown in the manuscript figure, the enabled risk layer assigned additional treatment more often in the controlled high-risk group than in the remaining authorized group. Removing the layer reduced both rates to zero. In both configurations, all cases entering the denominator of either rate had already passed the deterministic authorization check. For each of the two authorized groups, the rates of additional treatment with the risk layer enabled and removed are as follows. ## S6.5 Scope of the evidence

Within each authorized group, the measured rate gives the proportion of cases for which the classifier imposed additional treatment. Neither rate estimates authorization accuracy. For a proposed action that fails deterministic authorization, the classifier cannot supply the authority required for the action to execute. In the released records, the aggregate treatment rates are available, but the exact classifier hyperparameters and individual risk predictions are absent. Thus, the available records describe the observed treatment frequencies without supplying the inputs needed for an exact rerun of the classifier.
